Security

Careful controls, clear accountability.

Our security approach focuses on practical controls across identity, access, records, and operational resilience. We work to minimise risk through discipline rather than complexity.

How we protect care data

  • Access controls aligned to role and responsibility
  • Encryption for data in transit and at rest
  • Structured review of operational and vendor risk
  • Clear monitoring and incident response processes

Standards and assurance

CareBridge supports a robust governance model aligned to healthcare and privacy obligations in the UK, with a focus on practical, reviewable controls.
